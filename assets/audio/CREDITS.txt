==========================================

All game audio is original. Nothing was copied, sampled, or extracted
from any commercial game (including the Food Stars reference the palette
was only listened to, as a style guide).

MUSIC
-----
"Cafe morning" — original 56-bar bossa / cafe loop in C major, 112 BPM,
120 seconds, composed for Atelier Lueur as MIDI and rendered with
FluidR3_GM (Frank Wen, MIT License). Instruments used: nylon-string
guitar, acoustic bass, marimba, vibraphone, and GM percussion (maracas,
woodblocks, clave). The SoundFont is not bundled with the game; only the
rendered loop is shipped.

SOUND EFFECTS
-------------
Original physical-model syntheses written for this game (no third-party
sample packs were used):

  tap     dry plastic tick (band-limited click + short 2 kHz partials)
  drop    muted mid wood put-down (modal bar + soft attack noise)
  spawn   wet bubble / plop (falling-frequency sinusoid + tiny splash)
  merge   hard hollow woodblock / mallet clack + glockenspiel sparkle;
          one base recording, pitched per item tier by playbackRate
          (major-third / triad staircase, capped at ~3x)
  no      muted low wood thunk (invalid move; not harsh)
  empty   two descending muted thunks
  ready   two bright bells (order just became ready)
  coin    cash-register "ka-ching" + inharmonic coin clinks + chime
          flourish (also used for order delivered and coin rewards)
  quest   short rising mallet chime (a quest just completed)
  claim   mid chime for rewards that do not include coins
  shop    short bell + one clink (a purchase)
  open    rising filtered whoosh + hollow low thunk
  close   dry tick + falling whoosh
  unlock  wood body + rising sparkle
  level   longer rising bell flourish

Renderer: tools/render-cafe-audio.py (numpy / scipy, plus fluidsynth
for the music). No bookstore name appears in these files.
